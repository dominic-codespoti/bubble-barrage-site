How it plays

Two thumbs. Five minutes. One very armed dolphin.

Every dive is a five-minute survival run. Chain kills to fill the style meter, grab pearls to level up, and pick upgrades between waves.

🫧

Swim with one thumb, aim with the other

Drag anywhere to steer; the aim stick turns the head-mounted minigun independently, so you can flee forward while shooting backward. Tap AUTO for fixed-direction fire, or fine-aim every volley yourself. Portrait or landscape — the UI re-lays out live.

💨

Dash through danger

DASH bursts you in your swim direction with brief invulnerability, then recharges in under two seconds. Certain boons turn the dash itself into a weapon — Veyra’s Lunge Bite makes it an invulnerable attack run.

👑

Beat the Reef Warden

Elite hunters cut lanes through the reef at 1:30 and 3:00. At 4:00 the Reef Warden arrives — defeat it within 60 seconds or the run is lost. Win to unlock tougher tides and new starting loadouts.

Pick your court

Three emperors. Swear to one per dive.

Aurelius, Veyra and Nerath each visit mid-run in a full-screen ocean ceremony. Take your first emperor boon and that court becomes your patron for the dive — then stack its boons, evolutions and run-defining capstones.

Chill · Frost

Aurelius

The Rime-Crowned King

“Your little bubbles lack discipline. Allow me.”

Bubbles Chill longer and hit harder. Chilled hits ricochet between foes, hits leave Black Ice frost patches, and a Rime Mantle freezes whatever crowds you.

Mark · Crits

Veyra

Empress of the White Maw

“Stop fleeing. Choose your prey.”

A hopping Mark paints bonus damage on one foe at a time. Marked hits crit, harpoons pierce, and Lunge Bite bends your dash toward the Mark to bite clean through it.

Tendrils · Veil

Nerath

The Cathedral Below

“Little thunder-fish. The abyss is listening.”

Spectral tendrils strike on their own, drag foes in, and leave damaging ink pools. The Ink Veil swallows enemy orbs and slows everything inside it.

Rare Concords join two courts

Eligible runs can unite emperors: Cold-Blooded Royalty (Aurelius + Veyra), Maelstrom Pounce (Veyra + Nerath) and Frozen Veil (Nerath + Aurelius). Only one evolution or emperor capstone per run — choose the dive-defining one.

Win. Climb. Survive.

Tides I–V, then the endless Abyss

Win a tide to unlock the next. Harder tides raise enemy health, contact damage and spawn rate — and pay more Sand Dollars per dive.

Tide I · Sheltered

Rewards ×1

Standard enemy strength and pace. Win to unlock Tide II.

Tide II · Rising

Rewards ×1.25

Enemies +25% health, +15% contact damage, +12% spawn rate. Win to unlock Tide III.

Tide III · Storm

Rewards ×1.5

Enemies +50% health, +30% contact damage, +24% spawn rate. Win to unlock Tide IV.

Tide IV · Surge

Rewards ×1.75

Enemies +100% health, +70% contact damage, +50% spawn rate; hunters arrive 10 s early. Win to unlock Tide V.

Tide V · Maelstrom

Rewards ×2

Enemies +170% health, +130% contact damage, +80% spawn rate; hunters 20 s early, a faster Warden. Win to unlock the Abyss.

Abyss · Endless

Rewards ×2 + 15 Sand Dollars per minute

Plays as Tide V until the Warden falls — then the dive never ends. Every 30 seconds the pressure rises: faster spawns, tougher foes, and a hunter closing in. Survive as long as you can.

The sea fights back

Not just hordes. Hunters, ambushers, events.

New predators, new rules

  • Shellback crabs (from 1:00) shrug bubbles off their armoured front — flank them or aim the turret at their backs.
  • Pufferfish (from 1:30) inflate and spit slowing spine volleys. Pop them first for a style bonus.
  • Moray eels (from 2:00) flash a warning lane, lunge 7 m, then lie exposed — punish it.
  • Anglerfish (from 1:00) hover at range and lob dodgeable amber orbs. Your bubbles can pop the orbs mid-flight.

World events every 30–45 seconds

  • Giant clams crack open into a pearl burst worth about a level — sometimes a heal.
  • Sea mines chain-detonate in a 4 m blast. Shoot one, or lure the horde into it.
  • Bubble vents erupt into a 1.6× speed column that flings and stuns nearby enemies.
Progress that respects you

Reef Workshop + Wardrobe

Reef Workshop

Every dive — won, lost or abandoned — pays Sand Dollars: pearl value, survival time, hunters, the Warden, tide bonus. Spend them on capped, permanent stat lines with a free refund and an off switch. About one tide of power in total — skill still decides dives.

Wardrobe

Dress the dolphin in paints, gun finishes and bubble trails. Some are earned from the calendar and tide milestones; Sand Dollar styles sit in their own group. Sand Dollars are earned in play, never sold.

Fair by design

Free. No ads. Nothing pay-to-win.

Free download

The whole game is free. Optional permanent cosmetics only: five dolphin skins ($1.99 each or $4.99 for the collection, prices localized by the stores) plus a Supporter Pack. Nothing that makes you stronger is for sale.

No ads, no subscriptions, no coins

No advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no currencies to top up. Sand Dollars can only be earned by diving.

Offline play

Core single-player dives, settings and progression work fully offline. Only purchases, restore and platform cloud save need a connection.
