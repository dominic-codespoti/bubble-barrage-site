Privacy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Bubble Barrage is a free-to-download, single-player game. Core gameplay and game progression work offline. The app has no player account, online multiplayer, cloud save, advertising, or subscription. Optional permanent cosmetic skins are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play; purchase and restore flows require an internet connection and the relevant store account.

Information saved on your device

The game stores settings and progression locally on your device. These include audio and haptics preferences, selected loadout and tide, completed-run totals, best run results, and unlocked content. Some skins may be unlocked by gameplay goals and are recorded as part of local progression. Active runs are not restored after the app closes. Gameplay settings and progression are not sent to the developer as cloud saves.

Cosmetic purchases and RevenueCat

Paid options are permanent cosmetic skins only: Koi, Candy, Cosmic, Tropical, Royal, and a collection containing all five. Some skins are also earnable through progression. Purchases do not grant gameplay power. There is no coin wallet or in-game account.

The app initializes the RevenueCat SDK at startup and checks purchase entitlements. When the device is online, this startup check may send RevenueCat an anonymous app-user identifier and technical metadata, even if you do not make a purchase. The app does not require a Bubble Barrage account or ask you for your name or email address. RevenueCat may also process technical and app-use information to provide this service. When a purchase or restore is processed, RevenueCat may receive transaction information from the store, such as an Apple receipt or Google Play purchase token. This purchase processing is separate from game progress, which remains saved on your device. Read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Apple or Google handles the store transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. The game does not ask for or store payment-card details. The developer does not use purchase data for advertising or behavioral ad targeting. Purchase and restore screens need internet access; the rest of the single-player game remains playable offline.

Other services and the website

The game has no developer-operated multiplayer or cloud-save service. It does not include an advertising SDK. RevenueCat is used for purchase-entitlement checks; store providers separately process transactions. This policy will be updated if the app's services or data practices change.

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive ordinary technical request data (such as an IP address, browser details, and requested page) to serve and protect the site, according to its privacy statement.

Accounts and contact

The game does not require a Bubble Barrage account or ask players to provide a name or email address in the app. Apple or Google and RevenueCat process data as described in their own privacy notices.

Questions about this notice or purchases: domcodespoti@gmail.com. For help with a store transaction, contact the relevant app-store provider as well.
