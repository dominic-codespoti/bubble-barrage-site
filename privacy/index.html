Privacy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Bubble Barrage is a free-to-download, single-player game. Core gameplay and game progression work offline. The app has no Bubble Barrage account, no online multiplayer, no advertising, no subscription and no server operated by the developer. Optional permanent cosmetics are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play; purchase and restore flows require an internet connection and the relevant store account.

Information saved on your device

The game stores settings and progression on your device. These include audio, haptics, language and control preferences, selected loadout and tide, completed-run totals and best results, Sand Dollars (earned in play, never sold), Reef Workshop upgrades, daily missions and the login calendar, unlocked cosmetics, and a snapshot of a run in progress so it can be resumed once after you close the app. None of this is sent to the developer.

Cloud save, achievements and leaderboards

If you choose to use them, the game connects to your platform's own game service: Google Play Games on Android and Game Center on iPhone. Achievements and leaderboard scores are stored by that service under your platform account, and the platform handles the identifiers involved under its own privacy policy (Google, Apple). Optional cloud save keeps a copy of your game progress in Google Play Games Saved Games or in iCloud so it follows you between your devices. That copy is held by Google or Apple, not by the developer, and the developer cannot read it.

Reminder notifications

The game can show one local notification at most once a day, "Your daily missions are ready". It is off until you opt in, and you are asked only after you have played for a while. The notification is scheduled on your device; no notification token or content is sent to the developer. You can turn it off in the game's settings or in your device's notification settings.

Cosmetic purchases and RevenueCat

Paid options are permanent cosmetics only: dolphin skins (Koi, Candy, Cosmic, Tropical and Royal, or a collection of all five) and the Supporter Pack, which adds the premium paints and an exclusive gun finish. Purchases do not grant gameplay power. Sand Dollars cannot be bought.

The app initializes the RevenueCat SDK at startup and checks purchase entitlements. When the device is online, this startup check may send RevenueCat an anonymous app-user identifier and technical metadata, even if you do not make a purchase. The app does not require a Bubble Barrage account or ask you for your name or email address. RevenueCat may also process technical and app-use information to provide this service. When a purchase or restore is processed, RevenueCat may receive transaction information from the store, such as an Apple receipt or Google Play purchase token. Read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Apple or Google handles the store transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. The game does not ask for or store payment-card details. The developer does not use purchase data for advertising or behavioral ad targeting. Purchase and restore screens need internet access; the rest of the single-player game remains playable offline.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and choose Delete my data. After you confirm, the game erases its local saves, settings, progression, reminder and run snapshot, and it deletes the game's cloud save copy in Google Play Games or iCloud when cloud save is available. Purchases stay with your store account and can be restored afterwards. Achievements and leaderboard entries belong to your Google Play Games or Game Center account and cannot be removed from inside the app; manage them in your platform's game settings. You can also uninstall the game to remove everything stored on the device. For anything else, email the address below.

Other services and the website

The game has no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. RevenueCat is used for purchase-entitlement checks, and Google Play Games or Game Center for the optional features above; store providers separately process transactions. This policy will be updated if the app's services or data practices change.

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive ordinary technical request data (such as an IP address, browser details, and requested page) to serve and protect the site, according to its privacy statement.

Accounts and contact

The game does not require a Bubble Barrage account or ask players to provide a name or email address in the app. Apple, Google and RevenueCat process data as described in their own privacy notices.

Questions about this notice or purchases: hello@bubblebarrage.xyz. For help with a store transaction, contact the relevant app-store provider as well.
