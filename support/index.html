Bubble Barrage Support

Need help with the game or a cosmetic purchase? Email domcodespoti@gmail.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened. For purchase questions, name the skin/collection and which store you used; never send a password, full receipt, or payment details.

Offline play and saves

Bubble Barrage is free to download. Core single-player gameplay and local settings/progression work offline. There is no Bubble Barrage login or cloud save; closing the app does not resume an in-progress run. Some cosmetic skins can be earned through gameplay.

Cosmetic purchases and restore

Optional permanent cosmetic skins are available through the Apple App Store or Google Play: Koi, Candy, Cosmic, Tropical, Royal, and a collection containing all five. Purchases do not affect gameplay power; there are no coins, subscriptions, or advertisements. Purchase and restore require an internet connection and a signed-in account with the store that handled the transaction. The app uses RevenueCat's anonymous purchase-entitlement service and does not require a Bubble Barrage account. For billing/refund issues, contact Apple or Google. For in-app purchase/restore technical problems, email support with the product name and store.
