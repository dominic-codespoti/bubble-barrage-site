Bubble Barrage Support

Need help with the game or a cosmetic purchase? Email hello@bubblebarrage.xyz. Include your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened. For purchase questions, name the skin/collection and which store you used; never send a password, full receipt, or payment details.

Offline play, saves and your data

Bubble Barrage is free to download. Core single-player gameplay and local settings/progression work offline. There is no Bubble Barrage login. Optional cloud save uses Google Play Games or iCloud, and a run in progress can be resumed once after you close the app. To erase your data, open Settings and choose Delete my data; it clears local saves and the game's cloud save copy (see the privacy notice for details).

Cosmetic purchases and restore

Optional permanent cosmetics are available through the Apple App Store or Google Play: dolphin skins (Koi, Candy, Cosmic, Tropical, Royal, or a collection of all five) and the Supporter Pack. Purchases do not affect gameplay power; there are no subscriptions or advertisements, and Sand Dollars are earned in play, never sold. Purchase and restore require an internet connection and a signed-in account with the store that handled the transaction. The app uses RevenueCat's anonymous purchase-entitlement service and does not require a Bubble Barrage account. For billing/refund issues, contact Apple or Google. For in-app purchase/restore technical problems, email support with the product name and store.
